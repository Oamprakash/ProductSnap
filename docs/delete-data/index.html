Delete your ProductSnap data

ProductSnap for Android, by OAM Apps

ProductSnap has no accounts and never uploads your photos. It sends only anonymous usage and crash data to Google Firebase and ML Kit, as described in the privacy policy.

Request deletion

Email productsnap.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my ProductSnap data". Tell us roughly when you used the app and the phone model. We'll reply within 30 days and delete any data we can match to your request.

What is deleted

  • Usage events and app interaction data (Firebase Analytics)
  • Crash reports and diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • The random install IDs linked to them

What happens without a request

  • Analytics data is deleted automatically after 2 months.
  • Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Uninstalling the app stops all collection and resets its install IDs.
  • Your edited photos are only on your phone. Delete them from your gallery whenever you like.

Subscriptions

Google Play handles payments. To cancel Premium or see your purchase history, open the Play Store, then Payments & subscriptions.
