ProductSnap Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · ProductSnap for Android, by OAM Apps

Your photos stay on your phone. ProductSnap edits them on the device and never uploads them. We collect only anonymous usage and crash data, so we can fix problems and improve the app.

Who we are

ProductSnap is an Android app for online resellers, made by OAM Apps ("we"). If you have a question about this policy or your data, email CONTACT_EMAIL.

Your photos

Background removal, lighting, cropping, flaw marking and every other edit happen on your phone. Background removal uses Google's ML Kit, which runs on the device through Google Play services. Finished photos are saved to your phone's gallery. Photos you add to a batch are copied into the app's private storage on your phone and are deleted with the app. We never see, upload or store your photos.

What we collect

The app sends this data to Google services that we use. None of it includes your name, email address, photos, file names or anything you type.

All of this data is encrypted in transit.

What we don't collect

  • Your photos or videos
  • Your name, email address, phone number or contacts
  • Your payment details. Google Play handles payment, and the app only checks on your phone whether your subscription is active.
  • Your advertising ID. ProductSnap shows no ads.

There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Who processes the data

Google processes this data for us as a service provider, under its terms for Firebase and ML Kit. Google's servers may be outside your country, including in the United States. We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else for their own use.

How long we keep it

Analytics event data is kept for 2 months. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. After that they are deleted automatically.

Your choices and rights

We use this data on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, delete or object to the use of data about you.

Because the data isn't linked to your name or email, the simplest way to stop collection and reset your identifiers is to uninstall the app. To make a request, email CONTACT_EMAIL. We'll reply within 30 days.

Children

ProductSnap is made for adult sellers and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
