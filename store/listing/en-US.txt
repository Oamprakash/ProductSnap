TITLE
ProductSnap: Reseller Photos

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Remove backgrounds and fix lighting for eBay, Poshmark, Vinted and Etsy photos

FULL DESCRIPTION
Turn quick phone photos into clean, bright listing photos that sell. ProductSnap is a photo editor made for resellers on eBay, Poshmark, Vinted, Depop, Etsy and Mercari.

WHITE BACKGROUND IN ONE TAP
• Remove the background automatically and put your item on pure white, soft grey or any color
• Fix the cutout with a brush where the edge needs help
• Magic eraser for tags, stickers and stray threads

BETTER LIGHT, NO STUDIO
• Auto light fixes dark, yellow or grey photos
• Before and after compare, so you can see the difference
• Square, 4:5 and marketplace-ready crops

SHOW FLAWS HONESTLY
• Mark stains, holes and wear on the photo
• A close-up of each flaw is saved with your listing photos, so buyers trust you and returns go down

BATCH EDIT UP TO 30 PHOTOS
• Edit a whole haul at once with the same background and light
• Photos that need a look are flagged for you

PHOTO TIPS FOR SELLERS
• Good and bad examples for light, background, framing and angle
• A checklist of shots to take: front, back, brand label, size tag, details, flaws and measurements

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Every edit happens on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded.
• Saved to Pictures/ProductSnap, ready to share to any selling app

Every feature is free during launch.
